BallBattle

Code-generated ball challenges, every day on YouTube Shorts.

BallBattle is a YouTube channel of short, fully code-generated simulations: races, battles, ring escapes, territory wars and boss fights between a cast of ten ball characters. Every match is simulated from scratch, so every video is different.

BallBattle Studio is the private tool the channel owner uses to publish these videos. It uploads the day's videos to the BallBattle channel at their scheduled times and reads the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, subscribers) to see which formats work best. It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, and only with the owner's own YouTube channel.
