Privacy Policy

Brain Sprint Studio · last updated 11 November 2026

What Brain Sprint Studio is

Brain Sprint Studio is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Brain Sprint YouTube channel to upload the channel's videos, read the channel's statistics and reply to comments. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

YouTube API Services

Brain Sprint Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

  • Permission to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to that channel's public information and video list (youtube.readonly).
  • Read-only access to that channel's YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Reading comments on the channel's videos and posting the channel's replies to them (youtube.force-ssl). The tool does not delete, edit or moderate anything.

How data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish Brain Sprint videos and to show the channel owner their own statistics. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer, in files readable only by the owner. To draft titles and replies, the text of public comments on Brain Sprint videos (and the commenter's public display name) is sent to a self-hosted router (FreeLLMApi) that forwards it to third-party AI model providers; no other account data, tokens or statistics are sent to them. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No cookies or tracking are used, by the tool or by this website.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept locally for as long as the owner uses the tool and can be deleted at any time by removing the local files. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions; the stored tokens then stop working and are deleted locally.
