Brain Sprint

Quick quizzes and eye tests, every day on YouTube Shorts.

Brain Sprint is a YouTube channel of short, code-generated quizzes: find the different color, find the odd character, quick math, what comes next, count the dots. Every quiz has 8 levels that get harder, and every answer is shown in the video.

Brain Sprint Studio is the private tool the channel owner uses to publish these videos. It uploads the day's quizzes to the Brain Sprint channel at their scheduled times, reads the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, subscribers) and replies to comments. It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, and only with the owner's own YouTube channel.
