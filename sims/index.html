Satisfying Sims

Relaxing, code-generated physics simulations on YouTube Shorts.

Satisfying Sims is a YouTube channel of short, fully code-generated physics simulations with procedurally generated music: a ball that grows at every bounce, balls that multiply, pendulum drawings, falling sand.

Satisfying Sims Studio is the private tool the channel owner uses to publish these videos. It uploads the day's quizzes to the Satisfying Sims channel at their scheduled times, reads the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, subscribers) and replies to comments. It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, and only with the owner's own YouTube channel.
